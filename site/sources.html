What the public version leaves out

  • Edmonton lot outlines. The City of Edmonton does not publish them as open data: “As of November 1st, 2021, the City will no longer provide datasets with land parcel boundary polygon geometry” (City of Edmonton Open Data, Vacant Land – Industrial, stt5-pzaa). The full version uses licensed title mapping for academic work. Here, Edmonton lots are the City’s parcel points; cards that need a lot outline say so, and the seven Edmonton test fits are shown as images.
  • A licensed assessment list. The full version also counts commercial and apartment buildings from a licensed property assessment list as heat receivers; none of those records is published here.
  • Code and standard texts that are licensed are not reproduced; the tool cites them by clause.
  • The Edmonton test-fit drawings. Their plans show lot lines; the public version gives Edmonton results only (simplified outlines). They are in the full version.

Figures from other sources

Operator and industry figures (data-centre sizes, equipment) are labelled as claims where they appear. District-energy service areas are traced from City and utility maps and labelled as approximate.
