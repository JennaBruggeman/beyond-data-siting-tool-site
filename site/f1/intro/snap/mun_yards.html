Corner sites and the Director of Planning

Corner sites. On a corner site the exterior side yard follows the front yard rules (I-3 3.1.2.15).

The Director of Planning may reduce the front yard (3.1.2.14: stepped, varied massing and a more interesting form) and may decrease the side yard, considering the street network, loading and vehicle access, and open space (3.1.2.16).

A lot line that could read either way (about 45° off) is flagged: irregular lot, the Director of Planning confirms.

How each line was classed

Street edges are fronts. Edges roughly parallel to the front (within 45°) and farthest from it are rears. All others are sides. Each yard is measured square to its own edge, so on an irregular lot the buildable shape kinks.
