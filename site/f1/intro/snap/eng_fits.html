Why it stops
At 2.6 MW the generators go from 3 to 4 sets (3 needed + 1 spare). A set is 13.5 m long and the lot 30.9 m wide, so the sets stand one to a row and each one adds a row to the yard. The yard deepens from 17.5 to 23 m and the building footprint drops from 842 to 672 m². The 2 hall floors then give 1,343 m², below the 1,656 m² the hall, UPS and battery rooms need (637 m² per MW). The tool also stops at yards deeper than 21.7 m, the deepest it has tested: generator noise at the lot lines is not modelled.

How the size is found

The tool tries sizes from 0.1 MW up to the most the floor holds and keeps the largest where its set-up checks pass: building height, loading, plant and generators placed, fuel on site, noise, and a floor test for the hall, UPS and battery rooms.
