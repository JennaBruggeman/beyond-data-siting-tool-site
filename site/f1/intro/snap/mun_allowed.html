Is a data centre allowed?

What conditional means

A conditional use needs a permit decision: the Director of Planning or the Development Permit Board may grant it “either unconditionally or subject to conditions” or refuse it (ZDB s. 4.3.1).

Why no lot shows “permitted”

None of the 5 zones the tool lists for Vancouver gives a data centre a plain permitted use. In Vancouver “Bulk Data Storage” is conditional in every zone that lists it. Only lots in listed zones can be picked, so rezoning shows here only as the other path.
